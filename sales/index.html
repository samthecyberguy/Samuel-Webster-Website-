Simulated Campaign / Demonstration

Cold Email Campaign

A three-step practice sequence for a founder of a small B2B SaaS company managing technical customer support.

Read Sample Email Sequence
Objective
Start a conversation about a possible support bottleneck. This sample has no campaign performance results.
Tools & Methodology
Google Sheets for sequence planning; research the buyer's responsibilities, write a relevant opening, and use a low-pressure question.

1 / Initial Email

Subject: A question about customer support

Hi Alex,

When your team is building the product, who handles customer questions that need a little technical investigation?

I work in enterprise IT support and am offering part-time help with tickets, troubleshooting notes, and documentation for small SaaS teams. Is additional support capacity something you're considering?

Best,
Samuel

2 / Follow-Up

Subject: Re: A question about customer support

Hi Alex,

Following up on my note. I could help organize recurring questions, document reproduction steps, and prepare clear escalations so your developers have useful context.

Would it be helpful to discuss where requests currently get stuck?

Best,
Samuel

3 / Final Follow-Up

Subject: Re: A question about customer support

Hi Alex,

I'll close the loop for now. If support coverage or documentation becomes a priority, I'd be glad to learn about your workflow and see whether I can help.

Thanks for your time,
Samuel

Key takeaway: Each follow-up adds context while keeping the request simple and respectful. Alex is a fictional recipient; this is sample copy.

Sample ICP / Demonstration

Ideal Customer Profile & Lead Research

A sample qualification framework for finding small SaaS teams that might need additional support capacity.

View Sample Research Brief
Objective & Tools
Prioritize account fit using Apollo.io, LinkedIn, company websites, and Google Sheets.
Ideal Customer Profile
Small B2B SaaS companies with a live product, an identifiable customer-support process, and a potential need for part-time assistance.
Buyer Personas
Founder managing customer inquiries; support lead responsible for ticket flow and documentation.
Qualification Criteria
Relevant product, appropriate decision-maker, support needs to validate, compatible schedule, and approved tools and access. Public signals are research prompts, not proof of a buying need.

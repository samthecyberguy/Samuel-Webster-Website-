How It Works

Connect the steps you already take

A website inquiry can start a sequence of small actions, without someone repeating each step by hand.

  1. Website inquiry
  2. Customer information captured
  3. Google Sheet or CRM updated
  4. Instant notification sent
  5. Customer receives confirmation
  6. Follow-up sequence begins

I customize the steps around your business, including the tools you use, who needs to be notified, and when a person should take over.

Problems I Can Help Reduce

Less repetitive admin. Clearer handoffs.

Automation is useful when your business repeatedly performs predictable administrative steps.

  • Leads sitting unanswered
  • Repetitive copying and pasting between systems
  • Manually updating spreadsheets
  • Forgetting customer follow-ups
  • Re-entering information from forms
  • Manually creating tasks after new inquiries
  • Repetitive appointment or reminder messages
  • Files or requests being routed manually
  • Lack of visibility into where leads or requests stand
